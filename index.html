Quality Assurance

Every Device Gets a Second Look

Before reaching you, every refurbished device goes through a structured inspection and quality-check process.

See the full refurbishment process
  1. 01

    Device Inspection

    We record the model, serial number, storage and physical condition before anything is cleaned or opened.

  2. 02

    Battery & Performance Check

    Charge cycles and load tests are run so that underperforming batteries are replaced rather than resold.

  3. 03

    Display & Port Testing

    Screens are checked for dead pixels and touch response; every port is tested for charge and data transfer.

  4. 04

    Cleaning & Refurbishment

    Cases, keyboards and ports are cleaned, worn batteries swapped and cosmetic marks documented for grading.

  5. 05

    Final Quality Approval

    A final reviewer signs off the listing, condition grade and warranty period before the device is listed for sale.
