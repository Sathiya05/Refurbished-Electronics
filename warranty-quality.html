Our Refurbishment Process

Seven Steps, Same Standard Every Time

Devices that cannot meet the standard at any step are not listed for sale.

  1. 01

    Device Collection

    Trade-in devices are accepted and logged with their model, serial number and reported condition.

  2. 02

    Data Wipe

    A verified factory reset removes personal data before the device is graded or resold.

  3. 03

    Hardware Inspection

    Casing, screen glass, hinges and frames are examined for cracks, dents and impact damage.

  4. 04

    Functional Testing

    Every port, button, speaker, camera, microphone and wireless function is exercised.

  5. 05

    Cleaning

    Keyboards, ports and surfaces are cleaned so the device is ready to use out of the box.

  6. 06

    Quality Grading

    Condition, battery health and included accessories are recorded against a published grade.

  7. 07

    Final Inspection

    A second reviewer confirms the listing, grade and warranty before the listing goes live.

  8. If It Fails, It Is Not Listed

    Devices that fail a step go for further work or responsible recycling.

Transparent Condition

Condition Grades Explained

Every listing states the grade of the exact unit for sale. Minor cosmetic signs may be present depending on the selected grade.

Excellent

A

Minimal signs of use, clean and presentable.

  • No visible screen marks
  • Light wear on edges only
  • Battery health verified
  • Fully function tested

Very Good

B

A few light marks, no functional impact.

  • Faint marks on lid or back
  • No dents or cracks
  • Battery health verified
  • Fully function tested

Good

C

Visible everyday wear, working as intended.

  • Visible scuffs on body
  • Screen free of cracks
  • Battery health checked
  • All ports and buttons tested

Fair

D

Heavy cosmetic wear, still fully functional.

  • Scratches and dents visible
  • May include replaced parts
  • Battery replacement common
  • Function tested and graded
