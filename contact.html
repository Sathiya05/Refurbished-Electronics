Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about refurbished grading, testing, warranty and returns.

Still have a question?

A refurbished device has been previously owned, inspected, tested and graded by our team. It is sold with the condition grade, included accessories and warranty duration listed on the product page. “Refurbished” does not mean new — the selected grade tells you exactly what to expect cosmetically.

Every device goes through a checklist covering display, battery health, keyboard, ports, cameras, speakers, connectivity, storage and charging. Devices that fail any check are sent for further work or recycled instead of being sold.

We use four grades: Excellent, Very Good, Good and Fair. Each product page states the grade of the exact unit being sold, so you can compare like for like.

Warranty duration is listed per product because it varies by device and grade — you will see the period (for example 3 or 6 months) on each product page. It covers functional faults identified after delivery and does not cover physical or liquid damage.

Our demo policy allows a 30-day return window on eligible devices provided the device is returned in the same condition with the accessories supplied. Final terms are confirmed by the business before launch.
